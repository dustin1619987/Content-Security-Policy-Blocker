⚠ For testing only

Use this extension only for development, debugging, and authorized security testing on sites you control or have permission to test.

Disabling Content-Security-Policy removes a real defense-in-depth protection against cross-site scripting, clickjacking, and data-injection attacks. Do not use this extension for malicious purposes or on sites where you don't have authorization. Re-enable CSP (or just close the tab) when you are done.
